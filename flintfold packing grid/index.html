Privacy Policy for Flintfold Packing Grid

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Flintfold Packing Grid is a browser extension designed to help users compare two packing orientations, estimate fit counts, calculate wasted area, and export layout summaries as CSV files.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Flintfold Packing Grid extension.

1. Information We Collect

Flintfold Packing Grid does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The extension does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment information.

2. User Input and Calculations

Values entered into Flintfold Packing Grid are used only to perform the packing and layout calculations requested by the user.

These calculations are performed locally within the user's browser. Input values and calculation results are not sent to our servers.

3. CSV Exports

Flintfold Packing Grid allows users to export calculation summaries as CSV files. These files are generated for the user's own reference or planning purposes.

Exported files are created at the user's request and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

4. Browsing Activity

Flintfold Packing Grid does not monitor, collect, or record your browsing history, website activity, search history, or the content of websites you visit.

5. Third-Party Services

Flintfold Packing Grid does not sell or transfer user data to third parties for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing purposes.

6. Data Storage

We do not maintain a remote database containing personal information collected through Flintfold Packing Grid.

Any temporary information required for the extension's functionality is processed within the browser and is used only to provide the requested functionality.

7. Data Security

Because Flintfold Packing Grid does not transmit personal information to our servers, the extension minimizes the amount of user information exposed outside the user's device.

8. Children's Privacy

Flintfold Packing Grid does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the functionality of Flintfold Packing Grid changes or when necessary to comply with applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Flintfold Packing Grid extension, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the extension's official listing or website.
